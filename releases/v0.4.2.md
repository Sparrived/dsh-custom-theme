# dsh-custom-theme v0.4.2 — 2026-10-06

A background image now sits cleanly: the shell's own dividing lines and fades step aside while a zone
shows a picture, and a zone nested inside another no longer doubles its panel fill into a near-black
block. The zone picker also fills the width it is given.

**本次更新**：有背景图时 shell 自己的分割线与渐隐会让位，嵌套区域不再叠加底色（不再出现纯黑块），区域选择按钮铺满整行。

## Changed

- **The picture takes the shell's chrome with it.** While a zone shows a background image, three things
  that used to cut across it step aside: the 0.5px rule under the conversation header, the fade the
  transcript has above the composer, and the workspace list's fade at the bottom of the sidebar. They
  come back when the picture does — the rules live in the same per-pass sheet as the picture, so
  nothing is left behind to undo.
- **A zone inside another no longer stacks its panel fill.** The composer seat is a descendant of the
  conversation column, so both were painted: two 91% fills composite to 99%, which is the near-black
  block a new session showed in the middle of the column, around the welcome block and the input card.
  The inner fill is now written for the two together to come to the opacity you configured for that
  zone, and that zone no longer paints a second copy of the same picture.
- **The zone picker fills its row.** The six zones were content-sized tabs that stopped short of the
  row; they are now equal columns across it.

## Fixed

- **The reasoning pass asked for every think block on every mutation.** It ran that document query
  before checking whether the mode needed one, so a streaming reply that was not thinking paid for it
  on every chunk. The mutation records now decide first, so the query only runs when a turn actually
  changed. The four modes behave exactly as before.

## Upgrade notes

Nothing to change: update, restart the app, and the picture is drawn the same way with less over it.
Your picture opacity, blur, size and per-zone panel opacity are untouched — those decide how much
picture shows through, and this release is about what was sitting on top of it.

The maths-rendering delay reported against 0.4.1 is still open and is not addressed here.

## Verification

- `npm test` — 90 tests, passing.
- `npm run test:effects` — 6/6 steps, passing; needs no DSH and no token.
- `npm run test:working` / `npm run test:browser` — not run here: they need a live DSH window and a
  `DCT_TOKEN`.
- By hand: the three chrome targets and the nesting were read out of the shell's own sources — the
  header is the `.header` that holds a title row, the sidebar's fade is the `.fade` the workspace
  browser renders as a child of its `.treeBody`, and the composer seat is a descendant of the
  conversation column — and the nested-fill behaviour is held by a test asserting that the nested zone
  paints no layer of its own and writes a fill that no longer compounds the outer one.
